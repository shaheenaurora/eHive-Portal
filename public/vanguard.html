The room most founders never get.

At the heart of Vanguard is your Peer Advisory Board — six to eight founders, matched for calibre and mixed by sector, meeting in strict confidence. You bring a real challenge; you get the counsel of people who have faced it. Between sessions, you hold each other accountable.

Bought elsewhere, a board like this runs into tens of thousands a year. Here, it's where your membership begins.

The founding activation

Your first thirty days set your year.

In your first month, a Clarity Sprint maps where your business stands and what to do next — AED 2,500 of advisory, yours for AED 499 as a founding member. It's the paid first step that makes the membership yours from day one.

Priced at a fraction of what it returns.
