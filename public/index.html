Questions

What founders ask

Who is eHive for?

eHive is for Muslim entrepreneurs — founders, owners and operators — who take both their business and their principles seriously. You might be scaling a family business, launching a startup, or leading a profitable company and wondering what comes next. If you want a room built on shared ethics, you are likely a fit.

Do I have to be Muslim to join eHive Circle?

At launch, eHive Circle is for Muslim entrepreneurs so we can build an unambiguous founding culture rooted in Islamic business ethics. Over time we will widen to values-aligned operators who live by the same code of integrity, generosity and accountability.

What makes eHive different from other founder networks?

Three things. First, trust is our currency — every member is vetted, so the room starts at a higher standard. Second, the code is shared — integrity, generosity and accountability are not posters on a wall; they shape how referrals, advice and deals flow. Third, the rhythm is built around your life — family-friendly timing, values-led gatherings, and no code-switching required.

How much does it cost to get started?

Nothing to start. The Clarity Scorecard is free, and an initial consultation is a conversation, not a sales call. You only commit when you choose a paid consulting sprint or a Circle membership tier that fits your stage.

How are chapters and meetings structured?

Each chapter meets on a reliable rhythm — typically a monthly structured gathering for connection and business, plus smaller confidential peer groups that meet more frequently for depth and accountability. Times are set with family and life commitments in mind.

What happens after I apply?

We review your application, then invite you to a short conversation. If it is a fit, we admit you into the right chapter and tier. If not, we will tell you honestly — and usually point you toward the next best step, whether that is a consulting sprint or a future cohort.
