How it works

Local chapters. Real relationships. Measurable growth.

You join a local chapter that meets regularly — structured gatherings built for connection and business, small peer groups for accountability and depth, and events worth showing up for. Every part of it is designed around your calendar and your values.

Join a chapter

You join a local chapter of vetted, like-minded entrepreneurs. Every member is admitted by application, so the room is always people you can trust and want to know.

Meet regularly

Your chapter gathers on a reliable rhythm — structured meetings built for genuine connection, member spotlights, and business done properly.

Go deep in a peer group

Beyond the room, you join a small, confidential peer group — a handful of trusted members who meet to hold each other accountable, solve real problems, and grow together.

Grow, refer, and rise

As you contribute, you build a reputation and a network that compounds — real referrals, real relationships, and a path to lead within the community itself.
