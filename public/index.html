Questions

What founders ask

Who is eHive for?

Founders and operators building in the UAE — whether you're incorporating your first company, scaling an existing business, or looking for a community of peers.

How much does business setup cost?

It depends on jurisdiction (free zone vs mainland), visa requirements, and office needs. Use our cost calculator on the Business Setup page, or book a free consultation for a scoped quote.

Free zone or mainland — which is right for me?

Free zones offer 100% foreign ownership and tax benefits but may limit where you trade. Mainland companies can trade anywhere in the UAE. We help you choose based on your business model.

Is there any commitment to get started?

No. The pathfinder, scorecard, and initial consultation are free. You only commit when you choose a paid engagement or membership.

How long does setup take?

Many free-zone setups complete in 5–10 business days. Mainland and visa-heavy cases can take 2–4 weeks. We quote timelines before any work begins.
