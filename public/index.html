Questions

What founders ask

Who is eHive for?

Established and rising Muslim entrepreneurs who take both their business and their principles seriously — founders, owners and operators building something real.

Do I have to be Muslim to apply?

At launch, membership is for Muslim entrepreneurs so we can build an unambiguous founding culture. Over time we will widen to values-aligned operators who live by the same code.

What makes eHive different from other networks?

Trust is our currency. When everyone in the room shares the same ethical code, referrals mean more, relationships run deeper, and you never have to code-switch to fit in.

Is there any commitment to get started?

No. The Clarity Scorecard and initial consultation are free. You only commit when you choose a paid engagement or membership.

How are chapters and meetings structured?

Each chapter meets on a reliable rhythm — structured gatherings for connection and business, plus small confidential peer groups for depth and accountability.
