Our story

One founder. One missing ecosystem.

eHive began with a frustration its founder kept meeting first-hand: starting and growing a business in the region meant piecing together your own ecosystem — a formation agent here, a strategy firm there, and a community nowhere to be found. Every founder they met was quietly assembling the same puzzle, alone, from scratch.

Shaheen set out to build what had never existed: one ecosystem where a founder can set up properly, think clearly, and grow in good company — product and brand craft, consulting discipline, and the operational backbone engineered together, instead of scattered across a dozen vendors.

The result is eHive — three pillars, Business Setup, Consulting, and the eHive Circle community, engineered as one system. So that no founder has to assemble it alone ever again.
