Membership

One community. Room to grow into.

Whatever your scale, there’s a place for you — and every member, at every level, has one equal voice in the room.

Four tiersOne equal voiceBy application

The principle

Your tier reflects your stage, not your standing.

eHive tiers are designed around where you are in your journey and what you need from the community — not a ladder of importance. Every member is equal in the room, equal in their vote, and equal in the respect they’re owed. You choose the tier that fits how you want to take part.
