How it works

A simple model, built with care.

Local chapters, structured meetings, small peer groups, and events worth your time — designed end to end around how principled entrepreneurs actually build.

Local chaptersPeer groupsValues-aligned events

The model

Four steps to build with us.

01

Join a chapter

You join a local chapter of vetted, like-minded entrepreneurs. Every member is admitted by application, so the room is always people you can trust and want to know.

02

Meet regularly

Your chapter gathers on a reliable rhythm — structured meetings built for genuine connection, member spotlights, and business done properly. Every gathering is values-aligned and scheduled with your commitments in mind.

03

Go deep in a peer group

Beyond the room, you join a small, confidential peer group — a handful of trusted members who meet to hold each other accountable, solve real problems, and grow together. This is where the deepest value lives.

04

Grow, refer, and rise

As you contribute, you build a reputation and a network that compounds — real referrals, real relationships, and a path to lead within the community itself.

What makes it different

Built around your values, not around them

Every part of the eHive experience — when we meet, how we gather, the way business gets discussed, the partners we bring to the table — is designed so that you never have to choose between growing your business and honouring your principles. That design is the whole point.
