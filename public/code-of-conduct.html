Membership standard

The code we build by.

eHive is a community of trust. This code is how we protect it — for ourselves, for each other, and for everyone who joins after us.

What we expect

Show up with integrity. Leave the room better than you found it.

01

Keep your word

Do what you say you will do. Be on time. Be prepared. Your reliability is the foundation of every referral, partnership and friendship here.

02

Deal honestly

No hidden agendas, no misleading claims, no taking advantage of a brother’s trust. Business inside eHive is done on principle, not on pressure.

03

Give before you take

Generosity is our operating system. Share an introduction, answer a question, show up for someone else’s win. The score settles over time.

04

Protect confidentiality

What is shared in a peer group, a meeting, or a private conversation stays there. Trust collapses faster than it builds — guard it carefully.

05

Disclose conflicts

If you stand to benefit from a recommendation, say so. If a deal could create a conflict, bring it into the light early.

06

Hold the standard

We correct each other with respect, receive feedback with grace, and never let one another lower the bar just because it is easier.

In practice

What this means at our gatherings.

Our events are dignified, considered and welcoming. We pray on time. We avoid riba in our financial conversations. We treat every room as a place where every member can bring their whole self.

01

Prayer-aware

Meetings are scheduled around prayer times. Spaces for prayer are available and respected.

02

Ethics-first

We discuss money, growth and risk through the lens of what is permissible and beneficial — never what is merely profitable.

03

Member-led accountability

Every member has one equal voice. Concerns are raised directly and resolved transparently, one level up when needed.

If this is how you build, you belong here.

eHive admits members who share our standard. If that's you, we'd like to meet you.
